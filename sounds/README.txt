Sounds used by the app (any file here replaces the synthesised version):

  bark1.mp3 ... bark7.mp3   single barks; one is picked at random each time (any count works,
                            numbered from 1 with no gaps). A single bark.mp3 also works.
  growl.mp3                 played as a loop while the dog is awake (louder as the hand comes closer)
  bloom.mp3                 (not present) the rose sound; without it a soft synthesised swell plays

To use your own: replace these files, keep the names. Short, loud, no silence at the start.
